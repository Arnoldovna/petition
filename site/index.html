Nonsense Petition

A petition for absolutely nothing, rebuilt as a server-rendered Express 5 app that works without JavaScript. There is no live server, so here is the whole journey, recorded against the local demo stack.

demo video

what the video shows

  1. register step 1 · name, email and a password
  2. add a profile step 2 · age and city, both optional
  3. sign the petition step 3 · a signature drawn on the pad
  4. find yourself on the list step 4 · the last page of the supporters
  5. edit the profile step 5 · a new city
  6. delete the signature step 6 · on its own confirmation page

screenshots

run it yourself

With Node 26, pnpm and Docker, pnpm demo starts the production image with seeded supporters and prints a demo login. The README explains the rest.
